How a project runs

Four steps, start to support

Every engagement — a one-off fix or a full website and marketing build — moves through the same four stages, so you always know where things stand.

01

Understand

A detailed discovery call to map your current systems, everyday pain points and what success actually looks like for this project — all before a single rupee is quoted.

02

Plan

A clear, written plan lands in your inbox: exact scope, a realistic timeline, and pricing broken down line by line — nothing vague left to argue about later.

03

Build

Setup, development or campaign work begins with scheduled milestone check-ins, so you always know what's done, what's next and never end up in the dark.

04

Support

Full handover with documentation and login access, plus an ongoing support option if you want one — no lock-in contracts, no disappearing after go-live.
